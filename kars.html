    Translation notes

    Text styles

    One look per kind of text. Set a box the way you want, then press Save as the series style in its Text style panel. Every box of that kind without its own changes follows it.

    Stamps

    Images this series uses again and again, like your translated title logo. Add one once, then place it on any page and move or resize it like a box. It is included when you export.

    Shortcuts

    Add a box
    Drag over text. Shift+drag draws inside another box.
    Move or resize
    Drag a box, or drag its corners.
    Undo, redo
    Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, or click a step in History
    Delete, deselect
    Delete removes the selected box, or the page you clicked in the page list. Esc deselects.
    Pages
    [ ] or PageUp PageDown. Drag a page in the page list to reorder, or Alt+↑ ↓.
    Zoom
    + -, 0 to fit, Ctrl+scroll, or pinch
    Pan
    Scroll, Space+drag, middle-drag, or two fingers
    Tools
    A select, B brush, E eraser, I colour picker, T text box
    Original / translated
    V
    Find and replace
    Ctrl+H, in a page, the chapter or the whole series
    Heal (remove text on art)
    J, then paint over the text
    Save now
    Ctrl+S
